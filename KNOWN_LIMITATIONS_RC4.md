# RAS-Plot Replica RC4 Known Limitations

- RC4 is an engineering pilot, not a production or regulatory certification.
- Complex or alternative culvert configurations are not fully validated.
- Multiple-opening structures are not fully validated.
- Lateral structures require additional fixture-based validation.
- HEC-RAS versions and schema variations have not been exhaustively tested.
- Unknown vertical datum remains `UNKNOWN` and must be confirmed externally.
- SI processing is blocked; full SI conversion is not implemented.
- Actual AutoCAD/Civil 3D DXF compatibility remains unverified.
- FDT/FIRM generation remains disabled pending regulatory validation.
- DXF import and raster/DXF editing are outside this application.
- Regulatory deliverables require separate engineering review and approval.
